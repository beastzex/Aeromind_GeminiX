2. Do We Need to Train a Custom Model?

❌ NO — Absolutely Do Not Train a Custom Model

In the Best Apps and Agents track, training a model from scratch is counterproductive and discouraged:

  • NVIDIA Nemotron is Already World-Class: The Nemotron-3 models on Nebius Token Factory are massive foundation models trained on trillions of tokens with extraordinary reasoning, IATA aviation awareness, legal reasoning (EU261), and function-calling capabilities.
  • Judging Focus: The judges evaluate Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, and Idea Quality. They want to see agentic architectures, dynamic tool use, multi-modal physical grounding, and reliable user experience.
  • What We Build Instead: High-precision Agentic Prompt Engineering, dynamic JSON Schemas, Zod validators, and Tool Calling on top of Nebius Token Factory.

3. The 4 Differentiating Killer Features

Feature 1 Tavily Live Airport Threat Radar

The Problem: Airline APIs update delays 2–3 hours too late. Local strikes, security checkpoints, and FAA ground stops break out on news first.

The Solution: Backend calls api.tavily.com/search with targeted airport risk queries. Real-time news tickers alert travelers to strikes and ground stops, directly feeding a tavilyNewsRisk metric into our Disruption Score formula. (Directly clinches the $3,000 Tavily prize).

Feature 2 EU261 / US DOT Cash Claim Generator

The Problem: When flights are delayed >3h or canceled, airlines owe passengers €250–€600 / $650 by law, but 85% never claim it due to confusing forms.

The Solution: When delay exceeds 180m, Nemotron analyzes the route, carrier, and reason, evaluates legal eligibility, and generates an ironclad, formal legal claim letter ready to copy or download as a PDF.

Feature 3 Multilingual Desk Agent Flashcards

The Problem: Stranded at midnight in Tokyo Haneda or Paris CDG, deafening noise and language barriers make rebooking at the counter a nightmare.

The Solution: 1-tap "Show to Desk Agent" high-contrast flashcard in Japanese, German, French, Spanish, or Hindi invoking IATA Resolution 735d with PNR, accompanied by Deepgram audio pronunciation.

Feature 4 Digital-Twin Hotel & Car Ripple Re-plan

The Problem: Rebooking a flight is useless if your rental car desk closes at midnight and your hotel charges you as a "no-show".

The Solution: Approving a flight re-plan simultaneously generates a hotel late check-in dispatch email (via Resend) and auto-extends the car rental pickup grace period in one atomic approval.
